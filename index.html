FAQ

The questions I get most

Do I need to be technical?

No. That's the arrangement: you bring the business knowledge, I bring the AI. Nothing in the Audit requires you to understand how any of it works under the hood.

Do I have to give you access to my systems?

No. The Audit needs one conversation and a short intake form — no logins, no credentials, no IT involvement. Access only comes up if you decide to move into an Implementation, and even then it's scoped and on your terms.

I already tried AI and it didn't work — why would this be different?

Most failed attempts start with the wrong use case, or stop at a prototype that never reached the people doing the work. The Audit exists to find the right use case first — ranked by what it costs you today — before anything gets built.

What does this cost beyond your fee?

Nothing during the Audit. If you move forward, you buy and own any software licenses directly at list price. I don't resell tools and I don't take vendor commissions, so there's no markup and no reason for me to recommend anything you don't need.

Is my business too small for this?

I typically consult businesses with 20 to 200 employees, where a lot of work is still done by hand. That said, if your business is smaller or larger and you're ready to invest in this, I'd still love to talk.

What if I don't want to commit to a build afterward?

Then don't. The Audit stands alone and includes a quick-start plan your own team can run. Plenty of clients will use it purely to get clarity, and that's a fine outcome.

How does billing work on the Advisory Retainer?

It bills monthly on your signup anniversary date. To end it, give 30 days' written notice — the final month is prorated based on when the subscription actually ends.

Do you work with businesses outside the U.S.?

Not right now. AI SMB Consulting works with U.S.-based businesses only, in U.S. time zones.
