Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

AI Opportunity Audit ($2,500, one-time)

This is a fixed-scope engagement delivered within one week. Because the work (research, analysis, and roadmap creation) begins immediately after kickoff and the full deliverable is produced within days, the Audit is non-refundable once kickoff has begun (i.e., once we've received your intake information and started work).

If you haven't submitted intake information yet and want to cancel before work starts, contact hello@aismbconsulting.com for a full refund.

AI Implementation ($5,000–$15,000, scoped per project)

Terms for Implementation projects are set individually in the scoping agreement for that project, since scope, timeline, and payment structure (e.g., milestone-based) vary per engagement. Refund and cancellation terms will be specified in writing before the project begins.

AI Advisory Retainer ($3,000/month, subscription)

·Billing: Charged monthly on your signup date (e.g., if you sign up on the 10th, you're billed on the 10th of each following month).
·Cancellation notice: You may cancel at any time by emailing hello@aismbconsulting.com. Cancellation requires 30 days' notice — your subscription will end 30 days from the date we receive your cancellation request, not immediately.
·Final month proration: If your cancellation date falls in the middle of a billing cycle, your final month is prorated — you're only charged for the days of service actually used in that final cycle.
·No refunds for prior months: Past billing cycles that were fully used are not refunded. Proration applies only to the final, partial cycle.
·Reactivation: You can restart the Retainer at any time; it's treated as a new signup with a new billing anniversary date.

Free services

The AI Opportunity Preview call and the downloadable guide are free — there's nothing to cancel or refund, and no payment information is required to access either.

Disputes

If something about a charge seems wrong, email hello@aismbconsulting.com first — most billing questions are a quick fix. We'd rather resolve it directly than have you go through your card issuer.
