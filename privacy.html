Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

1. What this covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information AI SMB Consulting (a practice of Kella Brands, LLC) collects through aismbconsulting.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

You provide directly:

·Name and email address, when you request the free guide, book a call, submit the intake form, or purchase a service.
·Business information you share on an intake form or during a consultation (business type, team size, the operational details relevant to an engagement).
·Payment information, when you purchase a paid service — collected and processed by Stripe, not stored by us directly.

Collected automatically:

·Basic site usage data (pages visited, general location by IP, browser type) via standard analytics, if and when analytics tooling is added to the Site.

From third-party tools we use to run the business:

·Calendly, when you book a call — subject to Calendly's own privacy policy for the scheduling data it processes.
·Our email/CRM tool, when you join the guide list or nurture sequence.

3. How we use your information

·To deliver the free guide and Preview call.
·To deliver, invoice, and support paid engagements.
·To send the email sequence you opted into, and — if you separately agree — occasional updates afterward. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
·To improve our services and understand what prospective clients are looking for.

We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. How we share information

·With service providers who help us run the business (Stripe for payments, Calendly for scheduling, our email platform for the guide/drip sequence) — solely so they can perform that function, under their own privacy and security terms.
·If required by law, or to protect our legal rights.
·We do not share client-identifying business details publicly without your written permission.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business records, and comply with legal or tax obligations. You can request deletion of your contact information at any time (see Section 7) unless we're required to retain it for legal or accounting reasons.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including relying on reputable, security-vetted third parties (Stripe, Calendly, our email platform) for the data they process. No online system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

·Unsubscribe from any email at any time via the link in the email itself.
·Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by emailing hello@aismbconsulting.com.
·If you're in a jurisdiction with specific privacy rights (e.g., California's CCPA), those rights apply as required by law — contact us and we'll honor applicable requests.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and business professionals. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: hello@aismbconsulting.com
